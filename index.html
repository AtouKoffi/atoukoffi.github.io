Who I Am

About Me

I bridge the gap between rigorous mathematical theory and cutting-edge machine learning applications.

With a solid foundation in Mathematics (Bachelor's from the University of Lomé), I expanded my horizons by pursuing advanced machine learning education. I was honored with the prestigious African Masters in Machine Intelligence (AMMI) scholarship at AIMS Senegal.

I further refined my expertise through the intensive MITx MicroMasters in Statistics and Data Science, building robust knowledge in probability, mathematical statistics, and statistical modeling.

My professional career includes collaborative work with Facebook-Meta AI researchers (Laurens Van Der Maaten & Natalia Neverova) on Vision Transformers and NLP models. Today, I serve as a Data Scientist at the Togo AI Lab within the Ministry of Public Sector Efficiency & Digital Transformation of Togo, building AI-driven solutions to tackle real-world national problems.

As a Geospatial Data Scientist with expertise in building hybrid ML pipelines, I specialize in using deep learning models to extract rich feature embeddings from satellite imagery, enabling efficient and accurate label prediction using classic machine learning techniques.
